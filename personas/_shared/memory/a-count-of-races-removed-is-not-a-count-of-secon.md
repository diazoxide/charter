A COUNT OF RACES REMOVED IS NOT A COUNT OF SECONDS — I READ ONE AS THE OTHER AND FILED A WRONG ISSUE. I then filed #843 attributing test_frame_tmux_integration's 27.6s to server rebuilding and proposed applying the same fix. Measured in #864: the whole server-lifecycle premium is ~1.3s of a 29s module — 4%, NOT 82%. Two further claims in my issue also died on measurement: 'CI is slower' was backwards (21.5s on ubuntu-latest vs 28.2s on the workstation) and 'roughly 120s a mutation' was wrong (#840's own two sweep runs measured 84s). RULE: when reusing a previous fix's headline number, check what it COUNTED. A correctness fix's metric (races, births, retries removed) says nothing about duration, and a performance claim needs its own profile.
