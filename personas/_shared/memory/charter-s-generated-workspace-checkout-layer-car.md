# charter's generated workspace/checkout layer carries the RESTRICTIVE hal

_2026-09-10 15:27 · persistent_

charter's generated workspace/checkout layer carries the RESTRICTIVE half of the plane's permissions (permissions.ask, permissions.deny) and never permissions.allow — a grant put in force where nobody clicked for it is the failure the exclusion exists to prevent, and a restriction is its opposite (#942). A --local plane rule mirrors into a generated .claude/settings.local.json, not the committed sibling.
