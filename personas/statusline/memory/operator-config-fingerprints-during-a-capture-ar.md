# Operator config fingerprints during a capture are not proof on their own

_2026-09-11 03:18 · persistent_

Operator config fingerprints during a capture are not proof on their own: on 2026-09-11 the sha256 of ~/.claude/plugins/installed_plugins.json changed during a plain capture-frame.sh run (17 -> 18 charter@charter entries, mtime inside the capture window), and the new entry named another agent scratch plane (forge-git-env/plane1, installedAt 23:15:20Z), not the capture dir. Before blaming or clearing a capture, list entries whose lastUpdated is newer than the baseline and read their projectPath.
