# On a strip whose clicks resolve by COLUMN, any new per-tab field must be

_2026-09-04 12:26 · persistent_

On a strip whose clicks resolve by COLUMN, any new per-tab field must be FIXED-WIDTH and reserved on every tab, or it re-cuts the row the moment one tab's value changes and the cell the operator was about to press holds a neighbour's name. Two shipped instances: TAB_SPINNER takes the mark's existing cell; TAB_COUNT_W buys six cells on every workspace tab and draws blank when the count is zero.
