### Changed

- **Start fresh says when it interrupts a turn.** The question now says when the chat is
  mid-turn, or when its harness reports nothing so charter cannot tell. A chat started fresh also
  keeps its place in the strip at the next launch, not only in the window (#1246).

### Fixed

- **The keyboard has somewhere to go after Forget this chat….** It moves to the next Notice, or
  to the strip when no Notice is left, instead of landing on the page. A screen reader on a tab
  now hears its plane-updated mark too, because the tab is described by it (#1246).
