### Added

- **A settings gear on the project and the workspace you are in.** The project in front's tab and
  the focused workspace's tab each carry a gear that opens that one's settings. It shows only
  while the pointer is on that tab or the keyboard is on it, so the window stays quiet. Tab
  reaches it from its tab. Right-click and the palette still open settings too (SE-23, #1173).

### Changed

- **⌘, opens Settings where you are.** `⌘,` (`Ctrl+,` off a Mac), the app menu's Settings… and
  the palette's Settings… open Settings at the focused workspace's level, else at the project's,
  else at You when no project is open (SE-23, #1173).
