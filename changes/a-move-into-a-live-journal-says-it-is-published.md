### Fixed

- **A memory's Move says when a LIVE workspace's journal will publish it.** A move into a LIVE
  workspace's journal is published with the project, as a move into a persona's or shared memory
  is. The memory tab's Move help now names the LIVE workspaces among its stores, and a row's
  *Move to* submenu says it on that workspace's row. A move into a LOCAL workspace's journal
  says nothing of the kind. The Undo after a Move or a Delete is disabled while it is on its way,
  so a second press sends nothing (#1190).
