### Changed

- **The parts of who may dispatch to whom say the same thing.** A box ticked under a
  dispatch question is kept where the answer you press holds: in the task's workspace for
  an Allow, in any workspace for the wider one. The Notice that says a teammate's grant
  arrived, and a refusal kept while you were away, say what the other persona works with in
  the question's own words. Where that persona may dispatch onward only for work in one
  workspace, the sentence says which. Settings › Project › Dispatch says under a persona's
  name what its definition wants, and that this grants nothing. Reopening a finished task
  is refused, with the reason, where the project no longer lists the task's profile for its
  persona (#1502, #1504, #1505, #1506, #1507, #1509).

### Security

- **Who may dispatch to whom is answered and read in your own window only.** Every answer
  to a dispatch's question, Settings › Project › Dispatch, and the lists of a teammate's
  grants and of refusals kept while you were away are your window's alone.
- **Every yes is held to what it was shown.** An answer to a dispatch's question, to a
  teammate's grant, or to a refusal kept while you were away grants nothing where what it
  said has changed since, and you are shown it as it reads now.
- **A teammate's grant limited to one workspace is bound to the project's history** as every
  project grant is, and so is what Give back restores.
- **A chat nobody is at moves work into another workspace as another persona only under a
  grant that names the two**, by a handoff as by a task (#1483).
