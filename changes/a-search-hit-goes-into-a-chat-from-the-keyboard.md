### Added

- **A search hit goes into a chat from the keyboard.** In the Search tab's results, Shift+Enter
  on the hit you stepped to opens the chat picker, as _Add to a chat's context_ does in the
  preview. The chat you pick gets a reference to that file and line typed in, unsent, and what
  happened is said in the tab's status line. A hit from another project, or a project with no
  chat open, is said there too, and Escape closes the picker (#1151).
