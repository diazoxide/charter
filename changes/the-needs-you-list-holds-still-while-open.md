### Fixed

- **The ✋ list holds still while it is open.** A chat or a permission prompt that arrives while
  the list is open no longer moves the rows under the pointer, so a one-click answer lands on
  the row you aimed at. The list keeps the rows it opened on, in their order; one answered or
  withdrawn elsewhere meanwhile stays in its place, dimmed, with nothing on it to press; one you
  ignore from the list leaves it; and new ones show the next time it opens. The count on the hand still goes up at once (#1146).
