### Fixed

- **Every way into Settings puts the keyboard there.** The palette's Settings…, ⌘, and the app
  menu's Settings…, the gears, a doctor row's or a notice's "Fix it in Settings", and the level
  switcher now leave the keyboard on the current group in the Settings tab's group list, also
  when the tab was already open and is only brought forward. A dialog or drawer closed by such a
  link no longer hands the keyboard back to the button that opened it (#1206).
