### Added

- **What a task changed, and nothing of its siblings'.** A finished task's row has
  **Changes** (**Review changes** for a task on its own branch), and its report's line about
  what changed opens the same tab: the task's files, each opening its diff. A task on its own
  branch is offered **Merge…** into the branch it was cut from, a fast-forward or nothing,
  and **Discard branch…**, each asked first; a merge that does not apply cleanly changes
  nothing and says why. Only the person merges or discards: no chat can cause either. Two
  tasks of one chat working in one folder with no branch of their own are named, both of
  them, when the second starts and on the asking chat's tab (#1511).
