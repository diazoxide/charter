### Added

- **Open a file in your editor at the line you are reading.** A file shown in the light editor
  has *Open in your editor at line N*, for the line the cursor is on. Choose your editor on the
  Preferences tab: VS Code, Zed or a JetBrains IDE, through their own links, or `$VISUAL` /
  `$EDITOR`, started with `+line` and the file and never through a shell. Only a file the light
  editor would open is handed on (RC-20, #709).
