### Changed

- **One chat card at a time, and the next one comes at once.** A card the keyboard brought up on
  one row goes when the pointer brings up another's, and moving the pointer from one row to the
  next while a card is up shows the next card without waiting again. A finished task's row is
  one line too, as a chat's row is: its state is the mark, and the words, with how many times it
  was tried, are in the row's tooltip and read to a screen reader (#1687).
