### Added

- **You can answer a question a task put to the chat that dispatched it.** In a chat's
  Activity tab, a question its task is still waiting on has _Answer_: a small form with the
  question, a box, _Send_ and _Cancel_ (Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line). The task is
  handed your answer marked as the person's, and carries on. The chat that was asked is told on
  its next turn that you answered, with the question and your answer, and its own answer to
  that question is refused from then on. A question is answered once: if that chat answers
  first, you are told so and nothing of yours is sent. Your text is sent whole or not at all,
  never cut. The timeline says "you" answered. Only the purlis window can send an answer as
  yours: no chat's command, hook or file can (#1496).
