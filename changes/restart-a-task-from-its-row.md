### Added

- **Restart a task from its row in the Chats list.** A task with no tab of its own now has
  Restart chat on its row's menu, asked first on the row as its tab's menu asks. Why a restart
  was refused, or why it waits, is said on the row, so you need not open the task to read it
  (#1462).

### Fixed

- **The Notice after a sandbox setting changes hears more.** It asks again when the window comes
  back into focus, which catches a sandbox file written by hand or by another window and the
  policy an administrator sets, and when a task with no tab starts or ends (#1462).
