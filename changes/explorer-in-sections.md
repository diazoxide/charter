### Changed

- **Explorer is in sections: Workspaces, Repos and branches, and Files.** Each folds on its
  heading and stays folded on the next launch. Workspaces lists every workspace with the focused
  one marked, and a press focuses it as its tab on the strip does. Files shows the files of the
  picked branch or repo, so a branch is one row and its files are drawn once (#1677).
