### Added

- **Settings › You › This machine.** A new group lists what purlis remembers on this machine
  and lets you take each thing back. Recent projects have Forget, including one that has moved or
  gone. Pinned projects and workspaces have Unpin with an Undo that puts the pin back in its place.
  A pin to a workspace that is gone is listed as kept dormant, with Forget to remove it for good. Approved projects have Revoke, so the next
  open asks again. The update channel can be changed here as well. The core gains the calls to
  forget a recent project and to revoke an approval (ST-2, #1226).
