### Added

- **Clone a repo the workspace names but this machine lacks, from the window.** Each row under
  *Not cloned here* in the explorer has *Clone* on hover or focus and on its menu, the heading
  has *Clone all* when more than one is missing, and the bottom bar's row offers *Clone* on its
  menu. The palette has a *Clone* row per missing repo and *Clone all missing repos*. A row
  shows its clone under way, moves to the clones when it lands, and shows the core's reason and
  *Retry* when it fails (#1215).
