### Changed

- **A dismissed Notice stays dismissed until its cause changes.** Dismiss a dormant pin, or a
  chat that came back as a new chat, and it stays hidden after a relaunch on this machine. Once
  the cause has gone (the workspace is back, the chat came back as it was) the dismissal is let
  go, so it shows again if the cause returns (NO-2, #1229).
- **At most two Notices stand under the strip.** The most important come first, trouble before
  news, and the rest are behind "+N more", which opens them as a list (NO-2, #1229).
