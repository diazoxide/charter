### Changed

- **Preferences is now Settings.** The app menu's Settings… (`⌘,`, or `Ctrl+,`) and the
  palette's Settings… open a Settings tab in two columns: the level at the top, which is You
  for now, the groups on the left, and the chosen group on the right. Text holds the window's
  and the terminal's text size, and Editor holds your editor. Each applies as you change it, as
  before. A Preferences tab left open by an older version comes back as Settings (SE-16, #1166).
