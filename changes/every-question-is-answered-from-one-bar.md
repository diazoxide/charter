### Changed

- **Every question is answered from the same bar.** A dialog that asks something, such as
  deleting a workspace, removing a persona, approving a project or an extension, Push and Land,
  Save all, Make live, the doctor or About, now puts its buttons where the quit warning and
  Start a chat always did: in one row at the dialog's trailing edge, with the way out first and
  the act last (#1210).
