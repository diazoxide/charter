### Added

- **A todo opens in a tab of its own.** Clicking a todo in the Todos panel, or pressing Enter on
  it, opens it as a tab on its workspace's strip with its whole text, when it was opened, its
  state, and Mark done and Forget. A todo whose tab is already open brings that tab forward, and
  the palette has an *Open todo: …* row for each one (#1214).

### Changed

- **A todo's row no longer opens a card beside it.** Its tab holds the whole text instead, as a
  memory's and a persona's do (#1214).
