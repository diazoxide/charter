### Fixed

- **Settings no longer takes the keyboard after you have moved on.** A way into Settings that is
  still waiting for its tab is dropped on your next click or key press, so a tab drawn late
  does not pull the keyboard away from where you went. With two Settings tabs of one level side
  by side, the one in the focused pane takes the keyboard. Escape or Cancel on the start-chat
  dialog puts the keyboard back on the `+` that opened it (#1600).
