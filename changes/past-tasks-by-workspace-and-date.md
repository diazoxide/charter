### Added

- **Find past tasks by workspace and by date.** The Dispatches tab now narrows by the workspace
  a task worked in (or the project root) and by when it started: today, the past 7 days, or
  earlier. Opened from a workspace, it starts narrowed to that workspace, and _Every workspace_
  widens it. A task whose chat has closed is listed with its report for as long as this machine
  keeps its record (#1510).
