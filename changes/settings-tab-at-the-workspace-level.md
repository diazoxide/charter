### Changed

- **A workspace's settings are in the Settings tab.** Its level switcher now offers Workspace
  beside You and Project, with the workspace's settings in five groups: Live, Repos,
  Extensions, Appearance and Plugins; a group with nothing to set is left out. As at the
  Project level, each change is written to `workspace.json` as you make it, the last change has
  an Undo, and a value charter refuses is said beside its setting. The workspace's icon theme
  has a control here for the first time. Workspace settings… on a workspace's menu and in the
  palette opens Settings at that workspace's level, and the separate Workspace settings page is
  gone (SE-20, #1170).
