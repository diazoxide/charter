### Changed

- **The file tab is a tree beside a resizable preview.** *Browse the files* now shows the
  branch as the explorer's tree, folder by folder, instead of a flat list of its first 500
  paths. The divider between the tree and the preview can be dragged or moved with the arrow
  keys; the tab remembers where you left it when you close and reopen it, and across a
  relaunch. Opening the same branch's files again brings its tab forward. The preview draws
  images as images and markdown rendered, with a *Source* button for its text, and gives a
  binary file, one over 2 MiB, or an image over 40 megapixels a one-line summary with its
  size. The window also no longer follows any link off the app itself; links open in your
  browser. The type-to-narrow box went with the flat list (FM-2, #1105).
