### Fixed

- **An Activity line follows its chat while the tab is open.** When a chat on a session's
  Activity closes, its lines drop the button that showed it, and its Answer, and say "(chat
  closed)" after its name. Before, the button stayed until the tab was read again, and pressing
  it brought nothing forward. A chat restarted under another number keeps its button. The
  "also names" sentence is now drawn in the body text colour, with only its sign in the waiting
  colour, so it stays readable in the light theme (#1457).
