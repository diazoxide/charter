### Added

- **Focus the explorer on one branch.** *Focus on branch …* in a branch's menu turns the explorer
  into that branch's cockpit: its name, how many commits it is ahead of and behind the branch it
  was cut from, how many files it changed, and Merge and Done; then the chats working in it with
  their live status, and its files. Esc or the breadcrumb steps back out. Each window remembers
  its focus with its view tabs, and ⌘P looks in the focused branch first (FM-5, #1108).
