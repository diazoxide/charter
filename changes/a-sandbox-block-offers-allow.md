### Added

- **A sandbox block is never a dead end.** When the sandbox blocks a chat from reaching a host
  or writing a folder, the Notice on its tab now shows what it would allow, whole, and offers
  **Allow for this chat**, **Always allow…** (**Allow for me on this machine**, or for a host
  **Allow for everyone in this project**, as one of the project's own hosts) and **Keep
  blocked**. The chat restarts on the same conversation once its turn ends, and is told what
  was allowed so it retries by itself; where its harness does not say when a turn ends, the
  pane offers **Restart now** instead. A folder is offered only in the project or in one you
  list in Settings › Sandbox, judged as where it really is, so a link cannot widen it. Vault
  storage, purlis's own state, what only you approve and files later programs load are never
  allowed: the Notice says the way that works instead. For anything else purlis grants
  nothing, and the Notice offers **Start without the sandbox for this chat**, your own choice:
  it restarts the chat now, on the same conversation, and nothing it runs is confined until it
  next starts. Every grant is recorded in purlis's event log (#1342).
