### Changed

- **A task whose asking chat has closed ends at its report.** Its report is kept on its record,
  where the Dispatches tab shows it, and for the workspace, as before. The task's chat is then
  ended once its turn is over, the same as a task whose report reached its chat, and it no
  longer waits in your needs-you list. A task you started yourself from a tab, and a blocked
  task, still stay open for you (#1510).
